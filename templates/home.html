How it works

From headline to evidence, in three steps

No black box — just careful comparison you can follow.

1. Gather

We pull related stories from trusted archives and recent news, remove duplicates and open the articles properly.

2. Compare

Headline vs. story text are compared side-by-side. We look for matching details and language that signals a debunk.

3. Show

You get a verdict with a confidence bar and the original source — REAL UNSURE FAKE — ready to share.
